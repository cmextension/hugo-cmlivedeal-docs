##### Choose which share buttons appear with each deal, in the deal popup and on the deal page. Pinterest and Telegram are hidden by default; the other buttons are shown.

* **Facebook**: Show the Facebook share button.
* **X**: Show the X share button.
* **Pinterest**: Show the Pinterest share button. Pinterest also gets the deal's image. Hidden by default.
* **WhatsApp**: Show the WhatsApp share button. On a phone it opens the WhatsApp app; on a computer it opens WhatsApp Web.
* **Telegram**: Show the Telegram share button. Hidden by default.
* **Email**: Show a button that opens the visitor's email app, with the deal's title as the subject and its link in the message.
* **Device share**: Show a button that opens the device's own share menu, with every app the visitor has installed, for example Messenger, Zalo, Instagram or SMS. It appears only in browsers that support it, which are mostly on phones. Other browsers do not show it.

The `Copy link` box in the deal popup is always shown.

If you hide every button, the deal page shows no share buttons at all, and the popup shows only the `Copy link` box.
