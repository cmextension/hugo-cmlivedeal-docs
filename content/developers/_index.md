This part of the documentation is for developers who want to add their own features to CM Live Deal.

* [Events](/developers/events): listen to what CM Live Deal does, and change it from your own Joomla plugin.
* [Web Services API](/developers/api/): read and change deals, coupons, merchants and orders over HTTP, from a mobile app or another system.
* [Template overrides](/developers/template-overrides/): change the HTML of any CM Live Deal page or piece of a page, from your own template.
* [Styling](/developers/styling/): change colours, spacing and corners with CSS variables, and set up dark mode.
* [JavaScript](/developers/javascript/): the scripts, the data they read, the hooks they need in your HTML, and how to add your own.
