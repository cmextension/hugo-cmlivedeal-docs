---
title: Search for nearby deals
weight: 10
---
With the Search module, visitors can search for deals in all cities, in one city, or near their own location.

The `Your location` choice is added to the city list when **Geolocation service** is set in [General](/configuration/general/) options:

*   _MaxMind GeoLite2_ finds the visitor's location on the server, from their IP address. It works in every browser. You need to upload the GeoLite2 City database first. See [Get GeoLite2 City database](/cm-live-deal-search-module/get-geolite2-city-database/).
*   _HTML5 Geolocation_ asks the visitor's browser for their location. The visitor must allow it.

The deal list itself has a `Deals near me` button, a radius drop-down list and a map view too. See [Deals near me](/create-new-deal-in-front-end/deal-list/#deals-near-me).
