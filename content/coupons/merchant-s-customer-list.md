title: Merchant's customer list
weight: 60
---
Merchants manage the coupons of their deals on the `Customers` page. You can create a menu item for it in Menu Manager.

![/images/deal_frontend_menu_customer.webp](/images/deal_frontend_menu_customer.webp)

Open the menu item on the site. The `Your customers` page lists the coupons that customers have captured for your deals.

When a customer comes to redeem a coupon, use this page to search for their coupon code.

![/images/coupon_merchant_list.webp](/images/coupon_merchant_list.webp)

Click a coupon code to open a form where you can change the coupon's redeemed status and redeemed date.

![/images/coupon_merchant_form.webp](/images/coupon_merchant_form.webp)

NOTE: In CM Live Deal Configuration -> Permissions tab, remember to set `Edit Own` to `Allowed` for Merchant

![/images/configuration_merchant_allow_own.webp](/images/configuration_merchant_allow_own.webp)
